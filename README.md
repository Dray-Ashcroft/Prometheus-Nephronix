<h2 align="center">Prometheus Nephronix</h2>

<p align="center"><strong>Interactive Nephron &amp; Urine Formation Simulator</strong></p>

<p align="center">
Explore the excretory system from the body down to a single nephron, and watch urine form particle by particle.
</p>

🔸 **Features**

- Excretory System: Kidneys, ureters, bladder and urethra in male and female 3D models.
- Kidney L.S. & Blood Supply: Internal structure, and the routes of blood and urine.
- Nephron: Bowman's capsule, glomerulus, PCT, loop of Henle, DCT and collecting duct.
- Urine Formation: Filtration, reabsorption, secretion and the counter-current mechanism as particle animation.
- Regulation: ADH, RAAS and ANF, including arteriole constriction and dilation.

---

🔸 **Learning Objectives**

Understand how the kidneys filter blood, concentrate urine and regulate its formation (NCERT Class 11 Biology).

---

🔸 **Technology**

- HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript
- three.js, 3D Models & Animation
